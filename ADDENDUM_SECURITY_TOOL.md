## Addendum: Security / Penetration-Testing Tools

*Applies in addition to the Master Privacy Policy for apps that provide
security scanning, penetration-testing, or similar offensive-security
functionality.*

**Authorized use only:** this app is intended for use only against
systems you own or have explicit written authorization to test.
Unauthorized use against third-party systems may violate computer fraud
laws in your jurisdiction, and is solely your responsibility, not ours.

**Scan data:** targets, scan results, and findings you generate using
this app are stored under the same terms as other app data described in
the Master Policy, and are not shared with third parties.
