# Mason Privacy Policy

**App:** Mason, also called QuoteFlow Pro (Android package `com.bridgesai.mason`, web app at job-boss-app.lovable.app)
**Publisher:** Bridges AI Enterprises LLC, 2804 Mishawaka Ave, South Bend, IN 46616, USA
**Contact:** baesupport26@gmail.com
**Effective date:** October 17, 2026

Mason is a quoting, measuring and billing tool for trades contractors. This policy explains what Mason collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

## 1. What we collect

**Account information.** Your email address, your name, and your login credentials. We use these to create your account and sign you in.

**Business information you enter.** This includes your company name, address, phone number, logo, license details and the payment terms that appear on your quotes and invoices.

**Customer and job information you enter.** This includes customer names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses, job scopes, measurements, line items, prices, quotes, bid packets, deposit invoices and follow-up reminders.

**Employee and payroll information you enter.** This includes employee names, pay rates, hours and pay stubs you create in the payroll section.

**Photos and drawings.** These are jobsite photos you take or upload, photos of hand sketches, the drawings you make, and images Mason generates from them, such as before-and-after renders.

**Voice recordings.** When you use voice-to-quote, the recording is sent for transcription. We keep the resulting text, not the audio.

**Payment information.** Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. Mason never sees or stores your full card number. We store your subscription status and Stripe customer reference.

**Technical information.** Basic logs are kept for reliability and security. These include timestamps, error messages and the pages and features used.

Mason does not collect your precise location. Mason does not use advertising identifiers and does not show ads.

## 2. How we use it

We use this information to:
- run the features you use, such as building quotes, measuring, generating documents, sending bid packets and running payroll
- generate AI suggestions you ask for, such as unit pricing, material lists, renders and sales-assistant replies (you review every AI suggestion before it goes to a customer)
- process subscription payments
- keep the service secure and fix problems

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data, or your customers' data, to train AI models.

## 3. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers needed to run Mason, and only what each one needs:

| Provider | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lovable (hosting, database and file storage on Supabase) | All app data | Stores and serves your account and job data |
| Google, through the Lovable AI gateway (Gemini models) | Text, photos and sketches you submit to an AI feature | Produces AI pricing, takeoffs, renders and replies |
| ElevenLabs | Voice recordings from voice-to-quote | Turns speech into text |
| Stripe | Billing details and subscription events | Processes payments |
| Microsoft, through the Lovable connector gateway | Quote content you export | Creates Word documents when you use Word export |

**Your customers.** When you send a quote or bid packet, the customer receives a private link. Anyone with that link can view that document, so share links only with the intended customer.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

## 4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your account data and files right away. This includes quotes, customers, payroll records, photos, renders and marketplace templates. Copies in routine backups expire on the provider's backup schedule. Payment records held by Stripe follow Stripe's own retention rules.

## 5. Deleting your account and data

**In the app:** go to **Settings → Company profile & branding → Danger zone → Delete my account**, then type DELETE to confirm. This removes your login and all of your data immediately.

**Without the app:** see [How to delete your Mason account](mason-delete-account.md), or email baesupport26@gmail.com from the email address on your account.

## 6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each user's records are stored separately, and database access rules let only you read your own rows. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and fix issues quickly.

## 7. Children

Mason is a business tool for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit most of your information in the app at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or ask us to correct or delete it by emailing baesupport26@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. We will honor them.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app.
