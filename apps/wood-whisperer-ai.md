Wood Whisperer AI suggests woodworking projects, writes step-by-step build guides and sells shop jigs. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

## 1. What we collect

**Account information.** Your email address and password, or your Google account email if you sign in with Google. We use these to create your account and sign you in.

**What you ask for.** The project ideas you describe, such as the skill level, tools or style you want. We use them to generate ideas, build guides and project images.

**Build guides.** Guides you unlock are saved to your account so you can open them again.

**Purchases.** Build-guide unlocks are paid through Stripe. Wood Whisperer AI never sees or stores your card number. We keep a record of which guides you unlocked and a Stripe customer reference.

**Shop orders.** Jigs and other physical products are sold through our Shopify store. Your cart is kept on your device. When you check out, Shopify collects your name, shipping address and payment details under Shopify's own privacy policy. Wood Whisperer AI does not receive your card number.

**Technical information.** Basic logs for reliability and security, such as timestamps and error messages. The app stores your sign-in session and your cart in your browser's local storage.

Wood Whisperer AI does not collect your location, does not use advertising identifiers and does not show ads.

## 2. How we use it

We use this information to:
- generate the ideas, guides and images you ask for
- let you reopen guides you unlocked
- process payments and shop orders
- keep the service secure and fix problems

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data to train AI models.

## 3. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the app, and only what each one needs:

| Provider | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lovable (hosting, database and sign-in on Supabase) | Account data, saved guides and unlock records | Stores and serves your account |
| BAE AI Gateway and its AI model providers (such as Anthropic and fal.ai) | The text of your idea and guide requests | Writes ideas and guides and draws project images |
| Google | Your sign-in, if you choose Sign in with Google | Signs you in |
| Stripe | Your email and payment details | Processes guide unlock payments |
| Shopify | Cart contents, and at checkout your name, address and payment details | Sells and ships shop products |

AI requests are sent without your name or email address.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

## 4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your login, profile, saved guides and unlock records right away. Copies in routine backups expire on the provider's backup schedule. Payment records held by Stripe and order records held by Shopify follow their own retention rules.

## 5. Deleting your account and data

**In the app:** sign in, tap **Account**, type DELETE and confirm. This removes your login and all of your data immediately.

**Without the app:** see [How to delete your Wood Whisperer AI account](wood-whisperer-ai-delete-account.md), or email baesupport26@gmail.com from the email address on your account.

## 6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access rules let only you read your own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and fix issues quickly.

## 7. Children

Wood Whisperer AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Woodworking involves sharp tools; younger makers should work with an adult.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing baesupport26@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. We will honor them.

## 9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app.
