# How to delete your Mason account

**App:** Mason, also called QuoteFlow Pro (`com.bridgesai.mason`)
**Developer:** Bridges AI Enterprises LLC

You can delete your Mason account and all of its data at any time. You don't need to cancel anything first.

## Option 1: In the app (immediate)

1. Open Mason and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Company profile & branding**.
3. Scroll to **Danger zone** and tap **Delete my account**.
4. Type `DELETE` and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

## Option 2: Without the app

Email **baesupport26@gmail.com** from the email address on your Mason account. Use the subject line **"Delete my Mason account."** We will confirm the request and delete your account within 7 days, then reply to tell you it is done.

## What gets deleted

- Your login, meaning your email address and password
- Your company profile, logo and branding
- All quotes, line items, measurements, drawings, bid packets, deposit invoices and follow-ups
- All customer details you entered
- All employee and payroll records, including pay stubs
- All jobsite photos, sketch photos and AI-generated renders
- Marketplace templates you created and template purchases you made
- Your subscription record in Mason

## What may be kept, and for how long

- **Payment records at Stripe.** Deleting your account cancels any active Mason subscription billed through Stripe, so you will not be charged again. Stripe keeps the records of past charges as payment and tax law requires. If you subscribed through Google Play, cancel the subscription in the Play Store as well.
- **Backups.** Deleted data can remain in encrypted provider backups until those backups expire on their normal schedule. It is not restored into the app.
- **Documents you already sent.** Copies of quotes or packets you sent to customers, such as downloaded PDFs or emails, stay with those customers.
