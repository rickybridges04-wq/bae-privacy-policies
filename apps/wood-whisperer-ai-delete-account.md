# How to delete your Wood Whisperer AI account

**App:** Wood Whisperer AI
**Developer:** Bridges AI Enterprises LLC

You can delete your Wood Whisperer AI account and all of its data at any time.

## Option 1: In the app (immediate)

1. Open Wood Whisperer AI and sign in.
2. Tap **Account** at the top of the page.
3. Under **Delete account**, type `DELETE`.
4. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

## Option 2: Without the app

Email **baesupport26@gmail.com** from the email address on your account, with the subject line **"Delete my Wood Whisperer AI account."** We will confirm the request, delete your account within 7 days, and reply when it is done.

## What gets deleted

- Your login, meaning your email address and password or your Google sign-in link
- Your profile
- All build guides saved to your account
- Your record of guides you unlocked

## What may be kept, and for how long

- **Payment records at Stripe.** Stripe keeps records of past charges as payment and tax law requires. Deleting your account does not refund past purchases.
- **Shop orders at Shopify.** Orders you placed in the shop are kept by Shopify for fulfilment, returns and tax.
- **Backups.** Deleted data can remain in encrypted provider backups until those backups expire on their normal schedule. It is not restored into the app.
