# How to delete your LedgerSnap account

**App:** LedgerSnap, also listed as Receipt Genius Pro (`com.bridgesai.ledgersnap`)
**Developer:** Bridges AI Enterprises LLC

You can delete your LedgerSnap account and its data at any time.

## Option 1: In the app (immediate)

1. Open LedgerSnap and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Account**.
3. Under **Delete account**, tap **Delete my account**.
4. Type `DELETE` and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away, and an active subscription billed through Stripe is cancelled so you are not charged again.

## Option 2: Without the app

Email **baesupport26@gmail.com** from the email address on your account, with the subject line **"Delete my LedgerSnap account."** We will confirm the request, delete your account within 7 days, and reply when it is done.

## What gets deleted

- Your login, meaning your email address and password or your Google sign-in link
- Your profile
- Your receipts and receipt photos
- Your cash expenses and mileage trips
- Your team memberships and invitations you sent
- Your subscription record in LedgerSnap
- Teams you own where you are the only member

## What may be kept, and for how long

- **Shared team records.** If you own a team that has other members, ownership passes to another member, and the records you added to that team stay with the team.
- **Payment records at Stripe.** Stripe keeps records of past charges as payment and tax law requires. If you subscribed through an app store, cancel there as well.
- **Accounting software.** Bills already synced to QuickBooks or Xero stay in that software.
- **Backups.** Deleted data can remain in encrypted provider backups until those backups expire on their normal schedule. It is not restored into the app.
