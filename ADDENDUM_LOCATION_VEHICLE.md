## Addendum: Location / Vehicle Diagnostic Apps

*Applies in addition to the Master Privacy Policy for apps that access
device location or connect to vehicle diagnostic hardware (OBD-I/II).*

**Location data:** if this app requests location access (e.g., to search
nearby properties or plan a route), that data is used only to provide
the requested feature and is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

**Vehicle diagnostic data:** if this app connects to your vehicle's OBD
port, it can read diagnostic codes and sensor data from your vehicle.
This data is used only to display diagnostics back to you and is not
shared with insurers, manufacturers, or third parties without your
explicit action.
