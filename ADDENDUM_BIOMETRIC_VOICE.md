## Addendum: Voice/Likeness Cloning Apps

*Applies in addition to the Master Privacy Policy for apps that create a
digital voice or visual clone/avatar of you.*

**What's different about this data:** this app processes a sample of
your voice and/or likeness to create a digital replica. Voice and facial
likeness data is treated as sensitive biometric-adjacent information in
several U.S. states (e.g., Illinois' BIPA, and similar laws elsewhere) —
if you are a resident of such a state, additional consent and disclosure
requirements may apply, and you should be given an explicit opt-in before
any sample is captured.

**Retention:** the underlying voice/image sample used to create your
avatar is retained only as long as needed to regenerate or update your
avatar, unless you request its deletion, in which case both the sample
and the generated avatar are deleted.

**No unauthorized use:** your voice/likeness clone is generated for your
own use within this app only, and is not used to train shared models or
made available to other users without your explicit action (e.g.,
publishing it yourself).
